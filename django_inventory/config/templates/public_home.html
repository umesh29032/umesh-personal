Our Story

The Craft Behind
Every Stitch

Kapil Enterprises is a kids' garment manufacturer — hosiery, comfortable pants and vibrant t-shirts, made on our own factory floor.

Every garment we ship starts as a roll of cloth chosen by hand. From there it moves across cutting tables, through the skilled hands of our stitching teams, and past a finishing bench where every piece is checked before it's packed. Nothing leaves the floor unseen.

We believe good garments come from good working conditions — our artisans are paid fair piece-rates for the work they actually do, tracked openly stage by stage. The same care that goes into the pay goes into the product.

Behind the scenes, our own in-house inventory system follows every meter of cloth and every production batch in real time — so when you order from us, we know exactly where your garments are and when they'll be ready.

"Every garment leaves our floor with a story we can trace — from roll to rack."
How a garment is made
  1. 1

    Fabric Selection

    Cloth rolls are inspected and logged — every meter accounted for before it touches a table.

  2. 2

    Pattern & Cutting

    Layered cutting by size and color, planned so fabric waste stays at a minimum.

  3. 3

    Stitching

    Skilled artisan teams assemble each piece, with work recorded at every stage.

  4. 4

    Finishing & Quality Check

    Every garment is inspected; anything less than right is corrected before it moves on.

  5. 5

    Pack & Dispatch

    Counted, packed and dispatched — with the full batch history behind every carton.

Powered by our in-house inventory system — every roll, batch and stage tracked end to end.
